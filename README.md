## Business Insights

Example findings:

### Trend Analysis
* Sales show an upward trend over time.
* Seasonal spikes occur during festival periods.
* Demand increases in Q4 compared to Q1.
### Forecast Insights
* Sales expected to increase by 10–15% next year.
* Inventory should be increased before peak months.
* Staffing requirements may rise during high-demand periods.
### Business Recommendations
* Increase inventory during forecasted peak seasons.
* Launch promotions during low-demand months.
* Optimize warehouse stock levels.
* Improve supply chain planning using forecasts.
